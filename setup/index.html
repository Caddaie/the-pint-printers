Before the event

Let's get you set up

What's in the box

  • The printing machine, ink already fitted
  • Mains power cable
  • Selfie camera with USB plug
  • Pre-paid courier return label
  • A stack of branded coasters for your guests

Keep the box and all the packaging. You'll need it to send the machine home.

  1. Find it a good home

    A sturdy table near a plug socket, away from taps, spills and direct sunlight. Lift the machine out of the packaging with both hands. It weighs 13 kg, so two people makes it easier.

  2. Plug in the power

    Push the mains cable firmly into the power port low down on the back of the machine, then into the wall.

  3. Plug in the selfie camera

    Its USB plug goes into the port on the back, just below the silver button.

  4. Switch it on

    Press the silver button on the back. The screen will light up after a few seconds.

  5. Connect to Wi-Fi

    Tap the settings cog in the corner of the screen, then Wi-Fi Setting. Choose your venue's network and enter the password. Venue staff will have it if you don't. A phone hotspot works too.

  6. Do a test print

    Print any design to check everything's working (see Print). No pint to spare? Lay a paper napkin flat over the top of an empty pint glass and print onto that instead. If it comes out crisp and centred, you're ready for the big day.

Our advice: set up and test the night before or first thing in the morning, not when guests are arriving. It takes ten minutes and means the first pint of the day prints perfectly. We would appreciate it if you could leave the coasters beside the machine too, so guests can pick one up and keep it.
During the event

Printing the perfect pint

  1. Pour a drink with a proper foam head

    The design prints onto the foam, so a full, level head is everything. Guinness: let it settle so you've got that solid creamy head. Coffee and cocktails should have a smooth, even top.

  2. Choose a design

    Gallery: your designs are already loaded, just tap one.

    Upload: tap Upload and scan the QR code on screen with your phone to send over any photo.

    Camera: tap Camera and strike a pose with the selfie camera.

  3. Set the glass size

    On the design screen, drag the cup slider until it matches the width of your glass, shown in cm. Use the table below, or measure the inside of the rim, rim to rim across the widest point.

  4. Centre the glass on the tray

    Give the tray a quick wipe if it's caught any splashes, then place the glass on the circular pad, right in the middle.

  5. Tap Print

    The machine does the rest in around 15 seconds. Leave the glass on the tray until the design appears and the glass lowers back down.

  6. Enjoy

    The ink is food safe, flavourless and completely edible. Cheers.

After the event

Packing up, nice and easy

  1. Switch off and unplug

    Press the silver button on the back to switch off, then unplug the power cable and the selfie camera.

  2. Give it a gentle wipe

    A soft, slightly damp cloth over the tray and the outside is plenty. No sprays, and never water inside the machine.

  3. Leave the ink where it is

    The cartridge stays in the machine. No need to remove anything inside.

  4. Tuck the cables into the machine

    Coil the power cable and pop it, along with the selfie camera, into the storage recess in the machine, just as you found them.

  5. Machine into its packaging

    Lower the machine into the polystyrene packaging exactly as it arrived, then place the top piece over it. A snug fit protects it on the journey home.

  6. Tape it up and label it

    Tape the box securely shut, then tape the pre-paid return label flat onto the outside.

  7. Drop it at a parcel shop

    Within 2 working days of your event, please. The return label shows which courier, and their website lists your nearest drop-off point. Most corner shops and supermarkets have one. That's it, all done.

Why the packing matters

The polystyrene isn't just padding, it's what keeps the machine safe (and your damage deposit intact). Please pack it exactly as it arrived, with nothing loose inside the box.

Your deposit: once the machine is back with us and checked over, we'll refund your damage deposit in full. We'll drop you a note the moment it's done.
Troubleshooting

If something's not quite right

It won't switch on

Check the power cable is pushed fully into the back and the wall socket is on (test the socket with another electrical item). Then hold the silver button for 3 seconds.

It won't connect to Wi-Fi

Double-check the password with venue staff. If the venue Wi-Fi is being difficult, a phone hotspot works just as well.

The upload QR code isn't working

Make sure your phone has signal or is on the venue Wi-Fi. The designs already loaded in the gallery will still print in the meantime. If it keeps happening, restart the machine with the silver button on the back.

The print looks faint or patchy

Usually the foam, not the machine. Pour a fresh drink with a fuller, smoother head and print within a couple of minutes. If every print is faint, gently wipe the cartridge nozzle with a clean, slightly damp paper towel, or change the cartridge (see below).

The print is off centre

Re-centre the glass under the print head and check the glass size setting matches your glass (see Print).

The foam sinks before it prints

Print sooner after pouring. Cold, freshly poured drinks hold their head longest.

Changing the ink cartridge

  1. Open the cartridge lid

    It's the flap on the top of the machine. Lift out the old cartridge.

  2. Register the new one

    On screen, tap the settings cog, then Cartridge, and enter the SN code printed on the new cartridge.

  3. Fit it

    Tear off the blue ribbon seal. Without touching the print head, slide the cartridge in print head down until it clicks. Close the lid and run a test print.
