The small print

Competition terms.

Key details
  1. Promoter: ThePP Ltd, trading as The Pint Printers, company no. 17567738, 20 Buckingham Road, Wilmslow, SK9 5LB. Contact: hello@thepintprinters.co.uk.
  2. Who can enter: UK residents aged 18 or over. Not open to the promoter's directors, employees or their immediate families, or anyone else connected with running the competition.
  3. How to enter: complete the entry form at thepintprinters.co.uk/win. Entry is free and no purchase is necessary. One entry per person per round: further entries in the same round from the same person or email address will be ignored. Entries made in bulk, automatically or by third parties will be void.
  4. Two rounds: round 1 opens when this page goes live in November 2026 and closes at 11:59pm (UK time) on Sunday 13 December 2026. Round 2 opens at midnight on Monday 14 December 2026 and closes at 11:59pm (UK time) on Sunday 10 January 2027. An entry counts for the round that is open when we receive it, and only that round. To enter round 2 you need to enter again. Entries received after round 2 closes will not be counted.
  5. The draws: one winner per round will be chosen at random from that round's valid entries, using a random number generator applied to the full list: round 1 on Monday 14 December 2026 and round 2 on Monday 11 January 2027. A round 1 winner cannot also win round 2.
The prize
  1. There is one prize per round, two in total. Each prize is one Premium package hire for a single wedding or private party (such as a birthday, engagement, hen or stag) held in the UK, worth £595. It includes up to 400 prints, design of up to six print designs, the machine for the event, a fresh ink cartridge, tracked delivery to your venue and a prepaid return label. Corporate and commercial events are not included.
  2. The event must take place on or before 31 December 2028, on a date when a machine is available. Dates are allocated in the order they are confirmed, so we recommend claiming your date early.
  3. As with every hire, a refundable damage deposit of £300 is taken before the machine is dispatched and returned within seven days of the machine coming back undamaged. Our standard hire terms apply to the hire itself.
  4. The prize is not transferable and has no cash alternative. If circumstances beyond our control make it necessary, we may substitute a prize of equal or greater value.
Winning and claiming
  1. We will email each winner at the address they entered with on their round's draw day. The winner must reply within 14 days to accept the prize. If they do not, or if they are not eligible, we will draw a new winner at random from the same round.
  2. We will announce the winner's first name and county on our Instagram (@thepintprinters). Anyone can request the winner's surname and county by emailing us within one month of the draw. If you win and object to this, let us know when you accept and we will discuss it with you.
  3. If you booked and paid for a hire with us between entering and your round's draw, and you win, we will refund everything you have paid for that hire and upgrade it to the Premium package.
The entrant offer
  1. Every valid entrant who does not win will be emailed after their round's draw with an offer of our Essential package made unlimited prints with £50 off the usual price, when booked within 7 days: by Monday 21 December 2026 for round 1 and by Monday 18 January 2027 for round 2. Our standard prices and hire terms apply. The offer cannot be used with any other offer.

  3. We may cancel or change the competition if circumstances beyond our control require it, and will tell entrants if we do. We may disqualify entries that break these terms.
