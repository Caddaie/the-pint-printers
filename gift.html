How long is the voucher valid?

Two years from the day you buy it, for any of our hires: weddings, parties and corporate events anywhere in the UK. Dates are subject to availability, so the sooner they book, the better.

What if the voucher is worth more than the hire?

Any unused balance stays on the code until it expires. Vouchers can't be exchanged for cash.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. Email us within 14 days of buying and we'll refund you in full, as long as the voucher hasn't been used.

What if the voucher email doesn't arrive?

Check your spam folder first. If it's not there after a few hours, email hello@thepintprinters.co.uk and we'll resend it straight away.

Can someone else use it?

Yes. The voucher can be passed on to anyone. Whoever quotes the code when booking can use it.
