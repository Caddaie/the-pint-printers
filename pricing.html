Payments

How paying works.

Clear terms, no surprises. Here is the whole thing in three cards.

£150 secures your date

A £150 booking fee confirms your booking and takes your date off the market. It is non-refundable, and it comes off your total.

Balance 60 days before

For weddings and parties, the balance plus a refundable £300 damage deposit is due 60 days before your event. Corporate bookings, and any booking made within 60 days of the event, are paid in full upfront.

Deposit always comes back

The £300 damage deposit is refunded within seven working days of the machine arriving back in good condition. Cosmetic wear and tear is on us. It is also always returned in full if you cancel.

Pricing questions

The money questions, answered.

How much does it cost to hire a pint printer in the UK?

£395 to £595 for parties, £495 to £695 for weddings, and £750 to £1,050 for corporate events. The price is set by the number of prints included: roughly 200, 400 or 600 depending on the package. Every package includes the machine, ink, your designs loaded, tracked delivery and a pre-paid return label.

Why is the price based on prints rather than guests?

Because prints are what you actually use. Some guests print one pint, others print five. Choosing by prints means you pay for what your event will realistically get through, not a per-head estimate. As a rough guide, the machine prints about 30 to 40 pints an hour at a busy bar.

Are there any extra costs on top of the hire price?

No hidden ones. The hire price covers everything listed in the packages above. The only other money that changes hands is the refundable £300 damage deposit, held alongside your booking and returned within seven working days of the machine arriving back in good condition.

How do payments work?

A £150 booking fee secures your date. For weddings and parties, the balance plus the refundable £300 damage deposit is due 60 days before your event. Corporate bookings are paid in full upfront, with an invoice available on request. Bookings made within 60 days of the event are paid in full upfront.

What happens if we need to cancel?

The £150 booking fee is non-refundable. Cancel more than 60 days before your event and any balance already paid is refunded in full. Inside 60 days the hire fee is non-refundable. The £300 damage deposit is always returned in full on cancellation.

Can we add more prints on the day?

The cartridge fitted for your event covers your package's print count with headroom built in, so you will not hit a hard stop mid-evening. If you expect a particularly thirsty crowd, choose the next package up or mention it on your enquiry and we will advise.
